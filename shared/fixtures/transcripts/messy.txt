so yesterday we talked about the cache layer rewrite. recap:

the old cache was a simple dict with no TTL. it leaked entries for
deleted documents. we agreed on:

1. Redis with a 15 minute TTL for hot documents
2. read-through pattern, no write-behind (too risky for now)
3. keep the in-process LRU as layer one, 10k entries max

the migration script lives at https://gitlab.internal.example.com/platform/cache-migration
and the RFC is https://wiki.internal.example.com/rfc/cache-layer-v2

we also decided the stale-while-revalidate window is 30 seconds. anything
longer and users complained during the experiment last quarter.

open items: whether to shard by tenant or by document id, and whether the
metrics dashboards need a new panel for hit ratio per layer
(dashboards: https://grafana.internal.example.com/d/cache/overview).

oh and someone asked about the old memcached tier — that's being
decommissioned, no migration needed, just delete the feature flag after
cutover.
